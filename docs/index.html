The loop

Every application teaches the next one.

Most tools stop at "generate a resume". Callback runs the whole funnel and closes the loop with a memory of what actually worked for you.

Capture

Paste a posting or its URL. Callback reads the page's structured job data (company, role, location) and strips hidden text and prompt-injection payloads before any agent sees it.

Analyze fit, honestly

Must-haves, nice-to-haves, keywords, red flags, and a calibrated 0–100 fit score with a one-line rationale. Gaps stay visible.

Tailor from your profile

A resume and a cover letter for this job only, grounded in your uploaded CV. Anything it cannot verify becomes a placeholder, never a fabrication.

you approve

Verify like an ATS

Exports clean PDF/DOCX, reads the PDF back as a parser would, checks headings, contact details and keyword coverage, and flags numbers your profile does not back up.

Apply yourself

Callback never submits anything. You send the application; it tracks the stage on a pipeline board.

you submit

Log the outcome

Screen, interview, offer, rejection, ghosted. Each outcome triggers a retro that proposes a lesson.

Approve what sticks

Lessons wait in Memory until you activate them. Active lessons feed every future draft and prep session.

you decide

Prep for the interview

Mock interviews from the real job description, scored on a rubric with one concrete fix per answer. Weak answers come back as spaced-repetition drills.

Each job in the pipeline carries its own resume, cover letter, prep pack and interview history, tagged with the stage you practiced at, so nothing gets mixed up between roles.

Interview prep

Scored on real signals, not vibes.

Behavioral, technical and system-design modes. Every answer gets rubric scores and one concrete fix. Recurring weak dimensions become memory; weak answers become drills on a spaced schedule until they are strong. Prep happens before the interview, never during it.

Principles

The job market is a bot-versus-bot arms race. This is the honest side of it.

Auto-appliers spam and get accounts banned. Real-time interview copilots get people disqualified. Callback is the alternative: fewer, better-fit applications with authentic materials, and prep that makes you genuinely better.

01

Honesty by construction

Drafts come only from your real, stated experience. No invented titles, dates, metrics or skills. Export refuses a draft with placeholders left in it.

02

Human in the loop

Agents draft and organize. You review every document and submit every application yourself.

03

Postings are untrusted input

A posting guard strips invisible characters and "ignore previous instructions" payloads before any agent reads a listing, and shows you what it removed.

04

Prep, never cheat

Mock interviews, scoring and drills before the interview. No live assistance during one.

05

Local-first and private

Your resume, your salary history and the companies you are quietly talking to stay in ~/.callback on your machine. The server binds to localhost only.

06

Sanctioned access only

Polite fetching that respects robots.txt. No scraping job boards, no auto-apply, no terms-of-service games.
